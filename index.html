Phase 1 • 8 Minutes

What Are Forces? ⚙️

In science, a force is simply a push or a pull acting upon an object. Forces are measured in units called Newtons (N). Whenever an interaction stops, the objects no longer experience the force.

Push vs. Pull

Push: Applying force to move an object away from you (e.g., closing a door, kicking a soccer ball).

Pull: Applying force to bring an object closer to you (e.g., pulling a wagon, opening a drawer).

Applied vs. Non-Contact Forces

Applied Force ($F_a$): A direct contact force where a person or another object pushes or pulls an object.

Non-Contact Force: A force applied without physical contact between objects (e.g., gravity pulling down an apple, magnetic attraction).

Phase 3 • 12 Minutes

Push Your Thinking: Balanced vs. Unbalanced Forces ⚖️

Objects rarely experience only one force. The combination of all forces acting on an object is called the Net Force ($F_{net}$).

Balanced Forces ($F_{net} = 0\text{ N}$)

Forces pulling in opposite directions with equal strength cancel each other out. Result: No change in motion (object stays still or continues at constant velocity).

Unbalanced Forces ($F_{net} > 0\text{ N}$)

One force is stronger than the opposing force. Result: The object accelerates in the direction of the stronger force.
